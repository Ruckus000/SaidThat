## What is unblocked now

- Build and revise every in-scope feature under `apps/mobile/`.
- Use Room Beacon as the primary visual/interaction direction and Private Relay as the equal access and recovery countermodel.
- Use the simulation-backed content-state map, Design DNA, tokens, proposal spec, and demo spec as implementation guidance.

- A paid or moderated direction-validation study.

- Signed Direction or Handoff reviews.
- An editorial pipeline for fixture-only content.

- Current native iOS and Android device evidence for accessibility, lifecycle, offline behavior, sensors, and performance.
- Two distinct human editorial approvals plus retained source evidence before any authentic public-figure card can play.
- Any legal, platform, or privacy clearance needed for a non-fixture release.
