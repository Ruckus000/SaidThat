# Room Beacon proposal specification — simulation-backed draft

## Purpose

Give an evaluator a compact, honest explanation of the local-first MVP: one phone, a group-visible prompt, a blind holder who commits once, and a neutral post-answer truth review. This proposal does not assert market demand, participant comprehension, legal clearance, accessibility conformance, or release readiness.

## Required proposal sequence

1. **Problem and boundary:** explain that pre-reveal content is a game prompt, not a live social post or verified public claim.
2. **Primary ritual:** show Room Beacon's shared statement, persistent game beacon, one holder commit, and post-answer review.
3. **Countermodel:** show Private Relay's protected shutter as the equal tap-first, access/recovery route.
4. **Trust contract:** distinguish fixture-only, fabricated-for-game, authentic (editorial future), disputed, source-unavailable, and removed content; only reviewed records can ever become production playable.
5. **Failure handling:** show exactly-one-commit, protected background recovery, corrupt-deck withholding, and local minimised report queue behavior.
6. **Evidence posture:** label the direction as simulation-backed owner decision; list human/native/editorial evidence still required before release.

## Claims permitted in this draft

- The local MVP is account-free, offline-first, fixture-only, and has no live social feed or public upload path.
- The reducer test suite deterministically protects duplicate commits, private handoff, interruption, corrupt decks, and bounded reports.
- Room Beacon is the selected primary visual direction; Private Relay is a retained countermodel.

## Claims prohibited

- “Users understand,” “accessible,” “validated,” “safe for parties,” “legal,” “source-verified,” or equivalent outcome claims without the missing evidence.
- Any authentic public-figure presentation, social-post mimicry, or testimonial.
