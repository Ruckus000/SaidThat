# Direction comparison study runbook

**Status:** Preparation only — do not conduct or record a session until the plan is signed and the preflight checklist is complete.

## Preconditions that require accountable humans

- A product owner, research lead, accessibility lead, and editorial owner are named and accept their responsibilities.
- The editorial owner approves the fixture's actual study cards and confirms the two-person review path.
- The research lead freezes the small/large iOS/Android devices, primary/stress distances and angles, platform text size, lighting/noise conditions, and capture procedure in `shared-content.json`.
- The accessibility lead confirms recruitment and facilitation for participants who regularly use screen readers or non-motion controls, including VoiceOver and TalkBack coverage.
- The independent reviewer verifies this exact plan and signs it with the external private key. The private key must not enter the workspace.

## Recruitment and session structure

Run six moderated **group sessions** with the proposed friend-group context. Counterbalance first exposure so Room Beacon and Private Relay are first for three sessions each. Include the role-appropriate accessible paths as dedicated participant evidence; do not substitute a sighted evaluator script.

Record one anonymized session ID per group in `results.json`. Keep individual comprehension responses in the approved research record and summarize only the minimal, anonymized evidence required by the schema. Do not record raw audio/video, names, statement text, source URLs, session transcripts, raw motion, or free text in the repository.

## Moderator sequence

1. Read only the prepared neutral prompt; do not explain roles or controls.
2. Run the candidate's unaided-start scenario.
3. Run readability, control-recovery, and access scenarios from the frozen prototype.
4. Present all required review, reporting, offline, removal, and capture conditions.
5. Ask participants to explain state meaning and their next safe action in their own words.
6. Record structured outcomes against the signed kill criteria immediately after the session.

## Stop rules

- Stop testing the selected direction if a participant misunderstands the completed truth/game-context meaning under the signed plan's safety criterion.
- Stop and return to territory work after two unrecovered selected-direction primary-path failures.
- Stop any session that reveals a forehead card through an accessibility or handoff route, or uses unapproved editorial content.
- Do not reinterpret a failed or incomplete session as a pass. Keep it in the result record as required by the signed plan.

## After sessions

Only after six completed sessions, record anonymized structured results bound to the signed plan digest. Run the Direction validation checker with the external public key. A passing validation result remains evidence for a later explicit Direction review; it is not an approval or permission to implement.
