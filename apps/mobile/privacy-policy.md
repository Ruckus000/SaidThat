# Privacy Policy — Said That?

**Effective date:** <DATE>
**Contact:** <CONTACT EMAIL>

Said That? is a party game that runs entirely on your device.

**The app has no accounts, makes no network requests, and sends no data
anywhere.** It works in airplane mode, and it behaves identically whether or not
you have a connection, because it never has one.

## What we collect

Nothing. We operate no servers, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking.
We receive no information about you or your use of the app, and we could not
identify you if we wanted to.

There is no sign-in, no profile, no email capture, no contact list access, no
location access, no photo access, and no third-party SDK collecting data in the
background.

## What is stored on your device

Two things, both local to your phone and neither transmitted:

**1. Content reports.** If you tap "See a content issue?" on a reveal screen,
the app saves a record under `said-that:offline-report-queue:v1` containing:

- the identifier of the card you reported
- which of the three reasons you chose
- the deck version
- a timestamp
- a local run and round number

There is no free-text field, so you cannot enter personal information even
accidentally, and no identifier for you or your device is included. **These
reports stay on your phone.** There is no endpoint to deliver them to and no
background upload. If you want us to see one, you have to tell us yourself.

**2. Gameplay counts.** Under `said-that:playtest-card-stats:v1`, the app keeps
per-card tallies — how often a card was answered, answered correctly, skipped,
marked as the room's biggest laugh, and how many separate runs it appeared in.
These are counts attached to cards, not to people. No names, no scores per
player, no timestamps of play.

Both are erased when you delete the app. You can also clear them at any time
inside the app: **Settings → reset local session**, which asks for confirmation
first.

## Sharing gameplay counts, if you choose to

The settings screen has an export option. It opens your device's standard share
sheet with the card counts described above, and **you** choose where they go —
mail, messages, notes, a file, or nowhere. Nothing is sent unless you pick a
destination and send it.

The exported file contains only the deck version and the per-card counts. It
carries no identifier for you or your device. If you send it to us, we see the
counts and the address you sent it from — nothing more.

## Device permissions

The app asks for **motion access** only if you turn on the optional "tilt to
answer" control. It reads the accelerometer to detect a tilt gesture while a
round is on screen. The readings are used in the moment and never stored,
recorded, or transmitted.

If you decline, or never enable tilt, the game is fully playable by tapping.
Tap is the primary control, not a fallback.

## Children

The app has no accounts, no chat, no user-generated content, no purchases, and
no advertising. It collects nothing from anyone, including children.

## Beta testing via TestFlight

If you received this app through TestFlight, **Apple** — not us — collects
information as part of running TestFlight, including your email address, device
and OS details, crash logs, and how long you used the app. That is Apple's
processing under Apple's privacy policy, and it happens whether or not the app
itself collects anything, which it does not. Any feedback you submit through
TestFlight goes to Apple and is shown to us.

## Content about real people

The game includes genuine quotes from public figures alongside quotes written
for the game. Every card states which it is on the reveal screen, and genuine
ones show their source. If you are depicted and want a card removed, or you
believe a card is misattributed, contact us at <CONTACT EMAIL>. We can withdraw
any card so that it stops appearing in the next build.

## Changes

If a future version collects or transmits anything — crash reporting, analytics,
or any server feature — this policy will be updated before that version ships,
and the effective date above will change.
