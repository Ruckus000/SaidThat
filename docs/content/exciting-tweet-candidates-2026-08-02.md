# Exciting tweet candidates — editorial review (v2)

**Research date:** 2026-08-02 (aggregator-first rewrite)  
**Verdict pass:** Owner Y/N review applied; filters refined from those reasons.  
**Not legal clearance.** The 2026-08-02 research took wording from secondary
reporting. Everything that shipped was re-transcribed from Wayback captures
first — see the wording-provenance table below.  
**Partly shipped as of 2026-08-09.** Four of the eight keeps and ten decoys are
in `pop-voices`. #5, #12, #9 and #11 are not; their blockers are listed under
"Still not landed".

## Filter (refined from Y/N review)

A candidate must clear **all** of these:

1. **Fame 2026** — many Americans know the name or their work  
2. **Crazy statement** — read the text alone: does it sound unbelievable? Soft-quirky fails  
3. **Self-contained** — the forehead line works **without** feud backstory, meme lore, or a follow-up punchline. If you need “you had to be there,” cut it  
4. **Exciting, not merely awkward** — jaw-drop / absurd claim / ego meltdown with clear meaning. Cringe fails, social flops, and “Winning”-style slogans that only land with lore fail  
5. **Readable chaos, not gibberish** — unhinged is fine; confusing hashtag spam or opaque shade is not  
6. **Not brand-expected** — no “thought-provoking celebrity” lane (no Jaden)  
7. **Game-fun wild, not hate** — no hate / Hitler / antisemitism, child-targeting feuds, sexual-violence spectacle, suicide threats, medical-misinfo spectacles, or rape-case shock posts  

**Litmus (updated):** *Is this a crazy/wild statement that is exciting on its own, with no missing context?*

**Also:** Prefer short enough for a forehead card; long posts may need an agreed abbreviation that preserves meaning (see #5).

### What the Y/N pass taught us

| Pattern | Examples | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Keep clear absurd claims | Elon stock too high / no house; Ye elevator / immortals; SZA cockmeat; Trump nuclear / short-and-fat; Kim cavities | Self-contained wild claim |
| Cut context-dependent shade / mishaps | Rihanna tip feud; Harry Deep Heat | Needs backstory → fail |
| Cut boring / not exciting | 50 Cent grandma; Charlie Sheen Winning; Rita Ora RT flop | Awkward ≠ exciting |
| Cut confusing | Jim Carrey #BOING | Chaos must still parse |
| Cut ethics dumps | Cosby innocent; Nicki vaccine | Not party-fun wild |
| Cut typo-as-meme? | covfefe → N | Typo/gaffe alone is not a wild *claim* |
| Cut finance-news-as-hero? | Funding secured → N | Market-event lore ≠ forehead excitement (stock-too-high still Y — shorter, clearer absurd claim) |

## Decoys — the foil model was retired (2026-08-09)

**The nine drafted foils below cannot ship, and not because of their wording.**
Pairing a decoy to a specific authentic card is the structure the rubric names
as the fatal flaw of the previous deck:

> **S1 — The pairing structure is fatal.** Nearly every fabricated card is a
> same-figure companion to an authentic card… Within a run, seeing one makes the
> other free. — `editorial-rubric.md:564`

Three blocking, deck-level rules enforce that. A deck-level block fails the
whole build; there is no single card to withhold.

- **§3.4 rule 4** — "Never a rewrite of a neighbour."
- **P5** (`composition.mjs:134`) — no `formatFingerprint` twice in a deck.
- **P7** (`composition.mjs:165`) — two cards sharing a `figureId` must not share
  ≥3 content words: *"the decoy is a rewrite of the real one."*

The shipped 60-card deck has no `pairId`/`foilOf` field at all; its same-figure
cards are deliberately unrelated in content. The Kim foil ("I can **taste** when
someone has a cavity") trips P7 directly.

  are concrete, so both are `true`.
- Fingerprint exhaustion. See below.

Tell leakage sits at **50.0%** across 74 cards, inside the 58% warn line and the
62% block line, with no class markers and no feature separating the classes.

### The fingerprint vocabulary was extended

`FORMAT_FINGERPRINTS` had 10 free slots and this change needed 14. Four values
were added in `lib/schema.mjs`: `market-moving-aside`,
`renunciation-announcement`, `odd-talent-claim`, `notification-purge`.

That vocabulary is the real ceiling on deck size — P5 allows one shape per card,
deck-wide — so it is now **74 of 74 used**. Any further growth needs new entries,
and the comment added at the extension point says they may only be added when a
genuinely new shape arrives, never to make room for a card that could have
reused an existing one.
